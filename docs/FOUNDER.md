# Founder profile

The feasibility gates are computed against **this** person, not against an
average founder. An idea can have a real market and still be impossible for
you: the first step needs a licence you lack, a network you don't have, or
trust a stranger won't extend to you. Fill this in once, honestly, and
update it when your situation changes. The agent reads it before every
"check idea X".

Replace every `_…_` line. Short and concrete beats complete.

## Basics

- **Where you live and can legally do business:** _country / region, tax residency_
- **Languages you can sell in:** _native, fluent, workable_
- **Time per week:** _realistic hours, not hoped-for ones_
- **Technical depth:** _what you can build alone; what you'd need help with_
- **Runway:** _months you can work on this before it must pay_

> Guidance: the first gate ("can I take the first step?") is decided here.
> If the first customer must meet you in person, sign with a local entity,
> or hand you access to their money, write down whether you can do that.

## Trust assets

What makes a stranger believe you — the second gate.

- _Audience, followers, newsletter (with numbers)_
- _Professional credentials, licences, past employers in a niche_
- _Communities where you are known by name_
- _Shipped products, public track record_

> Guidance: "none yet" is a valid and useful answer. It rules out ideas
> where the first sale depends on reputation.

## Hard constraints (what you will NOT do)

Treat these like a budget: non-negotiable, and an idea that needs one is
closed at the gates.

- _e.g. no cold calling, no handling customer funds, no on-site work_
- _e.g. no products that need a regulated licence_
- _e.g. no paid ads before organic proof_

## Resources

- **Money you can spend before revenue:** _range per month_
- **Tools and data access you already pay for:** _APIs, SEO data, scraping, hosting_
- **People you can call on:** _co-builders, designers, domain experts_

## Implications for idea search (derived, not chosen)

Write these **after** the sections above, as consequences — not wishes.

- _e.g. "No local entity → skip ideas where the buyer needs a domestic invoice."_
- _e.g. "Sells in English only → target markets where English is the buying language."_
- _e.g. "Ten hours a week → no products needing same-day human support."_
