# Example: payroll-filing concierge for small foreign-owned companies

> **This is a fictional worked example** of a report that stops at the
> gates. The founder, numbers and market are invented to show the format.
> Delete this folder once you have real research.

**Date:** 2026-01-12 | **Founder profile:** fictional — solo developer,
lives abroad, English only, 15 h/week, no local entity in the target country.

## Question
Small companies owned by foreigners struggle with monthly payroll filings in
their host country. Could a done-for-you filing service with a simple
dashboard be a business for this founder?

## Gates (feasibility for this founder)

| Gate | Pass / fail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First step | **fail** | The first paid action is submitting a filing on the client's behalf. That requires a locally registered, licensed accountant to sign; the founder is neither and has no partner. |
| 2. Trust | fail | The client must hand a stranger abroad access to payroll and tax accounts. No credentials, no referrals, no audience in this niche. |
| 3. Small-customer arithmetic | pass | *Estimate:* a 5-person company pays a local firm around 150/month; at 80/month one client covers its own support time. |
| 4. Measurability | pass | Deadlines are monthly; success or failure is visible within weeks. |
| 5. Channel | unclear | Expat-founder forums exist; not checked, since gates 1–2 already failed. |
| 6. Regulatory risk | fail | Filing errors carry penalties; liability sits with whoever signs. |

## What we found
Two hours spent, all on gates. No market sizing was done — by design: the
first step is not one this founder can take, so demand does not matter.

## Competitors / what already exists
Not researched (gates failed).

## Assessment
The market may well exist. The blocker is the founder, not the market: the
product only works with a licensed local partner, and finding one is a
different business (a partnership hunt) with a different first step.

## Next step
None. Reopen only if `docs/FOUNDER.md` gains a licensed local partner.

**Verdict:** closed — the first step needs a local licence and client trust this founder cannot provide (gates 1, 2 and 6 failed).
